Our Story

A Legacy Built on Trust & Vision

Founded in 1991 by senior advocates Margaret Stackly and Richard Harmon, our firm was born from a singular belief: that exceptional legal counsel should be accessible to those who need it most, without compromise.

What began as a boutique practice in downtown Manhattan has grown into a formidable international law firm with offices across three continents. Our journey has been defined by landmark cases, transformative transactions, and an unwavering dedication to the principles that first guided us.

Our Mission

To deliver strategic, ethical, and personalized legal solutions that empower individuals, families, and enterprises to navigate complexity with confidence.

Our Vision

To be the most trusted and innovative law firm globally — recognized for integrity, impact, and the enduring relationships we build with every client.
